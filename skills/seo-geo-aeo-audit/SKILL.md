# SEO / GEO / AEO Audit

## Step 1: Confirm scope
Quick Audit (1 to 2 min, top issues) or Full Audit (5 to 10 min, all pages)?

## Step 2: Crawl
Homepage + robots.txt + sitemap.xml, then About, Services, Cases, Blog, Contact, FAQ.

## Step 3: Score
- **SEO**, On-Page, Technical, Content Quality
- **GEO**, E-E-A-T, Entity Clarity, Factual Density, Author Authority
- **AEO**, FAQ schema, HowTo, Question Headings, Direct-Answer Formatting

## Step 4: Output
Word doc + PDF with a priority matrix (Quick Wins / Strategic / Cleanup).
