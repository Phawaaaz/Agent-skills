# Research Synthesis

## Input

Interview transcripts, survey results, usability test notes, support tickets, NPS responses.

## Output

1. **3 to 5 Themes** (specific, not buzzword)
2. **Per theme:** 2 to 3 supporting quotes (sourced)
3. **Jobs-to-be-Done** behind each theme
4. **Top 3 testable hypotheses**
5. **Surprises** (low-frequency but high-signal findings)
6. **Recommended next steps** with priority
