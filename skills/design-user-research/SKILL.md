# User Research

## Plan

1. Goals & decisions this will inform
2. Method choice (interview / test / survey)
3. Participant criteria + recruitment
4. Sample size justification
5. Timeline + budget

## Conduct

1. Warm-up (3 min)
2. 5 to 7 tasks/questions in increasing depth
3. Per task: literal script + observation criteria
4. Wrap-up

## Bias Avoidance

- No leading questions ("How easy was it?" becomes "Tell me what you just did.")
- Silence-tolerance for participant thinking
- Open over closed where possible
