# Content Creation

## Universal Rules

- No buzzwords ("holistic", "empower", "unlock", "game-changer")
- Hook in first 2 sentences
- One claim per paragraph
- End with what reader does next

## Per Channel

- **Blog:** 1500-2200 words, H2/H3 structure, FAQ section, internal links
- **LinkedIn:** 90-140 words, line breaks, 1 hashtag tier
- **Email:** subject ≤50 chars, single CTA, scannable
- **Landing page:** AIDA, social proof above fold, single conversion goal
- **Case study:** scene → conflict → approach → results → what we'd do differently
